Transparency note · Full data sources & methodology →

Prototype ML model trained on simulated environmental stress data, designed to be replaceable with live environmental datasets. Scores and projections are scenario-based decision support, not official forecasts. Confidence is the share of 240 ensemble and input-uncertainty scenarios that agree on the risk level; it measures model consistency, not validated real-world accuracy.
